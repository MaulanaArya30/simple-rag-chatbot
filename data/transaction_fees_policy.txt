INTERNAL POLICY: TRANSACTION FEES AND PRICING TIERS
Document ID: FIN-POL-014 | Last updated: internal draft for demo purposes

1. Overview
This document describes how transaction fees are calculated for wallet-to-wallet
transfers, merchant payments, and cash-out operations on the platform.

2. Wallet-to-wallet transfers
Transfers between two personal wallets on the platform are free of charge when the
sender and recipient are verified users (KYC tier 1 or above). Unverified senders are
limited to a maximum of IDR 500,000 per transfer and incur a flat fee of IDR 1,000.

3. Merchant payments (QRIS and in-app checkout)
Merchant payments carry a Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) of 0.7% for QRIS transactions
and 1.5% for in-app checkout transactions. Micro-merchants (monthly volume below IDR
50,000,000) qualify for a discounted MDR of 0.3% on QRIS under the small merchant
support program.

4. Cash-out to bank account
Cash-out fees are tiered by amount:
  - Up to IDR 100,000: flat fee of IDR 2,500
  - IDR 100,001 - 1,000,000: flat fee of IDR 4,000
  - Above IDR 1,000,000: 0.5% of the cash-out amount, capped at IDR 25,000

5. Fee waivers
Fee waivers may be applied automatically during promotional periods or manually by
customer support for verified service disruptions. All waivers must be logged with a
ticket reference for audit purposes.

6. Currency and rounding
All fees are calculated in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and rounded up to the nearest IDR
100 to simplify display in the consumer app.
