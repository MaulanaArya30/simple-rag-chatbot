INTERNAL POLICY: REFUNDS AND TRANSACTION DISPUTES
Document ID: SUP-POL-031 | Last updated: internal draft for demo purposes

1. Eligible refund scenarios
- Duplicate payment caused by a network timeout on the user's device
- Merchant-confirmed order cancellation before fulfillment
- Payment sent to an incorrect QRIS code due to a scanning error, reported within 1
  hour of the transaction

2. Refund timelines
Merchant-approved refunds are processed back to the original wallet within 1 hour.
Refunds requiring manual fraud-team review (e.g. disputed wallet-to-wallet transfers)
are processed within 5 business days.

3. Dispute filing
Users can file a dispute from the transaction detail screen within 60 days of the
transaction date. Disputes must include a reason code and, where applicable,
supporting evidence (screenshot, order confirmation, chat transcript with the
merchant).

4. Chargeback handling for card-funded transactions
Top-ups made via debit card that result in a card-network chargeback are placed on
hold pending the issuing bank's investigation. The corresponding wallet balance is
frozen up to the disputed amount until the chargeback is resolved, to prevent
double-recovery of funds.

5. Fraud-confirmed transactions
If a transaction is confirmed fraudulent (e.g. account takeover), the affected funds
are reversed where technically possible, and the account is flagged for a mandatory
KYC re-verification before further cash-out is allowed.

6. SLA
Standard support SLA for a first response on a filed dispute is 4 business hours.
Full resolution SLA is 5 business days for cases not requiring law-enforcement
coordination.
