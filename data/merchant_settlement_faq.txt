FAQ: MERCHANT SETTLEMENT
Document ID: MER-FAQ-009 | Last updated: internal draft for demo purposes

Q: How often are merchant funds settled to a bank account?
A: Standard settlement runs once daily (T+1) for merchants in good standing. New
merchants (first 30 days) are settled on a T+2 cycle until their risk score
stabilizes.

Q: Can a merchant request faster settlement?
A: Eligible merchants (Tier 2 KYC, 90+ days active, no open disputes) can opt into
same-day settlement (T+0) for an additional 0.2% fee on the settled amount.

Q: What is held back from a settlement, if anything?
A: A rolling reserve of 1% of daily transaction volume is held for 30 days as a
buffer against future refunds and chargebacks, then released automatically if no
disputes are open against it.

Q: How are MDR fees deducted?
A: MDR fees are deducted at the point of transaction, so the amount settled to the
merchant's bank account is already net of fees. The merchant dashboard shows both
gross volume and net settled amount per settlement batch.

Q: What happens if a settlement fails due to incorrect bank details?
A: The settlement batch is retried once after 24 hours. If it fails again, the funds
remain in the merchant's platform balance and a notification is sent asking the
merchant to verify their linked bank account before the next settlement run.
