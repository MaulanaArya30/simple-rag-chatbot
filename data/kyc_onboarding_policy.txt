INTERNAL POLICY: KYC TIERS AND ONBOARDING
Document ID: COMP-POL-007 | Last updated: internal draft for demo purposes

1. KYC Tier 0 - Unverified
Created automatically on app download with a phone number. Wallet balance is capped
at IDR 2,000,000. No cash-out allowed. Valid for 30 days before the account is
restricted to read-only mode.

2. KYC Tier 1 - Basic Verification
Requires a national ID (KTP) photo and a selfie liveness check. Wallet balance cap
rises to IDR 20,000,000. Cash-out to a single linked bank account is enabled.
Verification is typically completed within 5 minutes via the automated OCR and
liveness pipeline; manual review is triggered if the confidence score is below 0.85.

3. KYC Tier 2 - Full Verification
Requires Tier 1 completion plus a proof-of-address document and a short video call
verification for balances or monthly inflow above IDR 50,000,000. Wallet balance cap
is removed. Required for users who wish to access credit or insurance products.

4. Merchant onboarding
Merchant accounts require a business registration number (NIB), a bank account in
the business name, and at least one authorized signatory who has completed KYC Tier
1 personally. Merchant accounts above IDR 500,000,000 monthly volume require an
annual re-verification cycle.

5. Re-verification triggers
Re-verification is triggered when: (a) a user's ID document expires, (b) suspicious
activity is flagged by the fraud model, or (c) a regulatory request is received.
Accounts pending re-verification are restricted from cash-out but can still receive
funds.
